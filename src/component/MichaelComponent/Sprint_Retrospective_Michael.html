## What can be done to improve the process?

  • Communication:
    • Improve communication between team members to ensure everyone is on the same page.
    • Implement regular check-ins or stand-up meetings to discuss progress and address any blockers.
  • Time Management:
    • Set clear deadlines and milestones for tasks to ensure timely completion.
    • Distribute workload evenly among team members to avoid burnout and ensure efficiency.
    • Lending help to team members who are struggling with their tasks to maintain overall productivity.
